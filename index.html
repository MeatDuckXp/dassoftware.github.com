The Playbook

01

Name the game

02

Design the system

Interfaces, SLOs, versioning, and change budgets. Make the happy path obvious and fast.

03

Decide & commit

Write the one‑pager. Lock scope. Sequence work for compounding wins, not perfect plans.

04

Ship and learn

Short cycles, strong retros, metrics that drive behavior. Cut what doesn’t move the needle.
